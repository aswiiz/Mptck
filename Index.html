Institution Identity

About Our Legacy

Model Polytechnic College, Karunagappally (MPTKY) is a premier technical institute established in 1997 under the Institute of Human Resources Development (IHRD), a Government of Kerala undertaking.

Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and affiliated with the State Board of Technical Education (SBTE) Kerala, the institute has spent nearly three decades cultivating a space where theoretical paradigms match actual laboratory benchmarks.

Regular 3-Year programs straight post-SSLC (10th standard).
Lateral Entry pathways straight into 2nd year for Plus Two/VHSE/ITI.

Advanced Labs Framework

Fully functional independent rigs configured across computer hardware, electronics signal processing, and electrical networks.

Digital Classrooms

ICT-integrated projection frameworks and an expansive central reference layout library.

The MPTC Advantage

Why Choose MPTC Karunagappally?

Delivering academic pathways alongside hands-on industrial experiences to engineer workplace-ready technology pioneers.

100% Placement Record

Distinguished track record driven by active industrial placement linkages. Graduates benefit from solid corporate placement tracks with students taking part in over 170 recruitment cycles during the 2025-26 academic year.

Expert & Qualified Faculty

Our seasoned technical mentors blend modern engineering paradigms with systematic student monitoring, steering classroom groups toward continuous analytical problem-solving abilities.

Modern Laboratories

State-of-the-art laboratory workshops loaded with custom instruments, advanced computing software tools, and standard diagnostic modules for high-level practical execution.

Industry On Campus (IOC)

Hosts two dedicated production units offering true industrial work exposure. Students gain real operational training by building industrial voltage stabilizers/inverters and engineering specialized software products.

Government Regulated

Operating under strict IHRD state policy frameworks, we uphold superior academic standards alongside transparent, affordable structures to ensure equitable access to elite technology tracks.

Excellent Academic Results

Our strict continuous evaluation processes enable classroom batches to regularly claim peak academic marks, honors lists, and top positions under state board metrics.
